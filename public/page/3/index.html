New paper published in Comp. Phys. Comm.! Fruit of a collaboration with researchers from UMET, we tested the Steinhardt Gaussian Mixture Analysis that was already employed in a previous paper and demonstrated that it can be used to identify defects in complex crystal structures. Great job from Jean Furstoss who recently got hired as CNRS researcher and Carlos R. Salazar our former Master student!
