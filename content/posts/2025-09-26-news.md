Very excited to announce that our projet NanoPHIA is now funded by the [PEPR Diadem](https://www.pepr-diadem.fr/)! We will receive 905k€ to work on the formation process in physical synthesis of nanoparticles by exploiting AI to couple characterisation, in-situ measurement and simulations. It will a collaboration with [CEMES](https://cemes.fr/), [INSP](https://w3.insp.upmc.fr/en/insp-en/) and [ILM](https://ilm.univ-lyon1.fr/) and 4 postdocs will be hired soon to do both simulations and experiments.
