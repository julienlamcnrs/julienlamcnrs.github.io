Can a system as thoroughly studied as the Lennard-Jones gas still reveal intriguing phenomena? In a collaboration with the [Laboratoire de Physique de la Matière Condensée](https://pmc.polytechnique.fr/?lang=fr) published in [Phys Rev Letters](https://doi.org/10.1103/n3b1-tgjv), we employed Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations and rare-event sampling to investigate liquid nucleation from the vapor phase. Contrary to the predictions of Classical Nucleation Theory, our results demonstrate that critical clusters nucleate at a density significantly different from that of the macroscopic equilibrium liquid. We rationalize these numerical findings using a two-variable nucleation theory that is both accessible and highly effective.
