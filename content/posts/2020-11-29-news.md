Happy to welcome two students who will start their 5 months internship by February 2021: Owen Fresse–Colson and Thomas Da Rocha. The first one will work on some technical developpments for our [Φ-LassoLars method](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2632-2153/abc9fd) and the second one will work on the [Seeding method](https://aip.scitation.org/doi/abs/10.1063/1.4939641) for binary crystallization. Special thanks to the [Licence Physique Parcours SPÉCIAL](https://www.univ-tlse3.fr/licence-physique-parcours-special-physique) of Université Paul Sabatier.
