New paper published in [Comp. Phys. Comm.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computer-physics-communications)! Fruit of a collaboration with researchers from [UMET](https://umet.univ-lille.fr/Plasticite/index.php?lang=fr), we tested the Steinhardt Gaussian Mixture Analysis that was already employed in a [previous paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-024-01371-x) and demonstrated that it can be used to identify defects in complex crystal structures. Great job from [Jean Furstoss](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jean-Furstoss) who recently got hired as CNRS researcher and [Carlos R. Salazar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zTfnKR0AAAAJ&hl=en) our former Master student!
